# 0003: Photos as the main capture

**Status:** Proposed. To be decided after the side-by-side test.

## Context

Photos offer higher quality and RAW files. Video offers dense viewpoints and easier
tracking, but is compressed and has lower resolution.

## Decision

Design around photos every 1–1.5 m. Shoot the same 100 m both ways and compare the
results before accepting this decision.

## Consequences

- Tracking across photos is harder, and it's the main risk to test.
- Video frames could later be supported as just another input.
